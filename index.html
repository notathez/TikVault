How it works

  • 1

    Export your data

    Go to TikTok settings and request your data. Download the archive.
  • 2

    Find like List.json

    Open the archive and locate the "Like list.json" file. This is the file you need.
  • 3

    Upload the file

    Upload the the JSON file to TikVault. All data is loaded instantly in your browser.
  • 4

    Browse & Enjoy

    Search, filter, watch and manager your liked videos with ease.
